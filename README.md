## 🚀 Overview

**Slingshot: Event Horizon** is a browser-based cosmic physics runner. Players pilot an advanced deep-space interceptor caught in the gravitational wake of a collapsing supermassive black hole. With primary propulsion offline, your only method of survival is a **Gravitational Tether Hook**—latching onto celestial bodies, entering orbit, and slingshotting through gravity wells at relativistic fractions of the speed of light ($c$).

Designed to stand out in hackathon judging, the game blends **addictive arcade loops** with **authentic orbital mechanics, generative synthwave music, and relativistic visual effects**.

## ⚡ Active Quantum Abilities

* 🛡️ **Quantum Phase Shift (`[SHIFT]` or Mobile Button)**: Makes your ship intangible for 3.2 seconds. Slice straight through star cores, asteroid clusters, and pulsar beams without taking damage!
* ⏱️ **Relativistic Time Dilation (`[F]` or `[Q]`)**: Slows cosmic time to 40% while preserving ship agility, allowing split-second orbital aiming through binary star corridors.

## 🌟 Celestial Systems & Hazards

* 🔵 **Blue Hypergiant**: Stable gravitational well and balanced slingshot strength.
* 🔴 **Red Supergiant**: Vast gravity radius for wide, sweeping turns.
* 🟣 **Dense White Dwarf**: Compact radius, hyper-G pull, and extreme ejection velocities.
* 💥 **Magnetar Pulsar**: Sweeping radiation beams and immense slingshot power.
* 🌌 **Binary Star System**: Twin stars orbiting a common barycenter! Slinging between both awards a **"DOUBLE GRAVITY ASSIST"** combo!
* ⚠️ **Unstable Supernova**: Pulsing unstable stars that test your quick escape timing.
* 🌀 **Wormholes**: Enter to trigger a $1400\text{ LY}$ hyperspace warp jump!
* 🪨 **Asteroid Clusters**: Hazards that damage shields unless phased through or dodged.
* ✨ **Photon Cores**: Refuel ion thrusters and recharge shields.
