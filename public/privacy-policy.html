COMPLIANCE & DATA PRIVACY

PRIVACY POLICY

APPLICATION: SLINGSHOT: EVENT HORIZON // LAST UPDATED: OCTOBER 2026

1. OVERVIEW & CORE COMMITMENT

Slingshot: Event Horizon ("the Game", "we", "us") is an offline-capable orbital physics simulator and cosmic runner. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or monetize any personal user data.

2. DATA COLLECTION & USAGE

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): The Game does not require account creation, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, or names.
  • Local Device Storage: Gameplay progress (pilot rank, high scores, discovered codex celestial bodies, unlocked ships, and custom settings) is stored exclusively on your local device using standard browser/client local storage. This data is never sent to any external server.
  • No Telemetry or Third-Party Tracking: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, analytic trackers, or fingerprinting scripts.
  • No Sensitive Permissions: The Android application does not request camera, microphone, precise location, storage, or address book permissions. Only INTERNET (for web rendering) and VIBRATE (for tactile haptic feedback) are declared.

3. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY (COPPA COMPLIANCE)

The Game is designed for all audiences and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personal information from children under the age of 13.

4. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES & CDNS

When running in web mode, public non-identifying graphical assets (such as Three.js WebGL library and open 3D model formats) may be streamed from verified content delivery networks (CDNs). These requests carry standard HTTP headers and do not track users across the internet.

5. DATA RETENTION & DELETION

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have complete control. Clearing your app cache, clearing browser storage, or uninstalling the Game immediately and permanently removes all saved game data.
